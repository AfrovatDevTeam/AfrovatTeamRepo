#### **Question1:Definition Of Software Governance**



##### **Software Governance is the framework of rules, processes and controls that guide how software is developed and managed. The purpose is to ensure software is high quality, secure and aligned with business goals.**







#### **Question2:Difference Between Software Governance And Software Ethics** 



##### **Governance Focus On rules, processes and controls in software development whereas Ethics Focus on moral principals and doing what is right for users and society**





##### **Question3:Four Key Elements Of Software Governance**



##### **-Quality Control-Ensure software meets standards** 

##### **-Security Management-Protects Software From Threats** 

##### **-Risk Management-Identifies And Reduces Risks** 

##### **Compliance-Ensures Laws, Policies , and standards are followed** 





##### **Question4:Why Insecure Software Is Both A Technical And Ethical Failure**



##### **It Is  a Technical Issue because it breaks security requirements** 

##### **It is a Ethical Failure because it exposes users to harm that could have been prevented**  





##### **Question5:A startup collects user location data without informing users because “it improves analytics.”**

##### **a) Identify two ethical principles violated. b) Identify one governance failure.**



##### **A) Ethical Principals Violated: Privacy and Transparency** 



##### **B) Governance Failure: Lack of user consent and proper data- collection controls** 











##### **Question6:Why is the statement “The system made the decision” not an acceptable ethical defence?**



##### **Because humans design , build, and control systems, so responsibility cannot be shifted to technology. Humans remain accountable** 







##### **Question7:POPIA CASE-Fintech data breach**



##### **A) Missing Controls: Encryption, access control, and proper data security** 



##### **B) Law Violated: POPIA in South Africa** 

##### 

##### **C) Lesson: Developers must always secure personal data and follow privacy laws** 





##### **Question8:Algorithmic Bias**





##### **A) Ethical principal violated: Fairness \& Non-discrimination** 

##### 

##### **B) Governance Mechanism: Bias Testing, Audits and transparent review of Algorithms** 





##### **Question9:Five Developer Responsibilities**



##### **-Write Secure Code** 

##### **-Protect User Data** 

##### **-Follow Standards \& Policies** 

##### **-Test For Bugs \& Risks** 

##### **-Report Issues.**



##### **Question10:Why Ethics Fill The Gap:**



##### **Ethics guides decision-making in situations where laws and policies are unclear or silent, helping people act responsibly.**

##### 

##### **Question11:Purpose Of COBIT:**



##### **COBIT provides processes and controls to manage IT effectively and align it with business goals** 





##### **Question12:Role Of KING IV In IT Governance** 



##### **King IV provides principals and guidance for boards to ensure IT supports business goals, is secure and ethically managed**





##### **Question13:Purpose Of PAIA** 



##### **PAIA ensures organisations manage information properly and provide access to records when requested, promoting and accountability** 





##### **Question14:How COBIT,KINGIV, and PAIA work together**



##### **-KING IV sets governance principals** 

##### **-COBIT provides IT processes and controls** 

##### **-PAIA ensures legal compliances with information access**

##### 

##### **Together, they support secure, ethical and well governed IT**





#### **Question15:Role Of Board Of Directors In IT Governance** 



##### **The board oversees IT strategy, approves policies, manages risks, and ensures compliances, providing overall governance and accountability** 



##### **Question16:Cloud Governance Risks**



1. ##### **Data Security and breaches**
2. ##### **Compliances With laws and regulations**
3. ##### **Loss of control over data and access management**  





##### **Question17:Audit Readiness in IT governance** 



##### **Audit readiness means heavy processes, records, and controls in place so IT systems and data can be easily reviewed and verified for compliance** 





##### **Question18:Balancing PAIA access with privacy** 



##### **Organisations should grant access to information as required by PAIA while protecting personal or sensitive data to maintain privacy**





##### **Question19:Why good IT governance supports innovation**



##### **Good IT governance provides clear processes, risk management and resource alignment, which allows teams to innovate safely and efficiently without causing security and compliance problems**





##### **Question20:Essay Question**





##### **Developers are responsible for creating software that is secure, reliable, and ethical. By following ethical principles like privacy, fairness, and transparency, they protect users from harm. Through IT governance frameworks such as COBIT and guidance from King IV, they ensure software aligns with business goals, complies with policies, and manages risks effectively. In the South African regulatory context, laws like POPIA and PAIA require developers to handle personal data responsibly and provide access to information when needed. Together, ethics, governance, and legal compliance make developers key custodians of trust in the digital economy, ensuring users can rely on technology safely and fairly.**
